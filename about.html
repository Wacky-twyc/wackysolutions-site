About

We are the translator in the middle

!WackySolutions LLC is an app development company. We are here for the person with a genuinely good app idea and no idea what to do next.

Twenty-five years of business IT, the unglamorous kind, for organizations where systems have to actually work on Monday morning. That history matters less for the code and more for the judgment.

About

Why we started

There is a gap in the app industry, and a lot of good ideas fall into it

On one side are people with real ideas: a way to fix something they deal with every day, a business they’ve run for years that should have an app. On the other side you have development firms with quotes 30 pages long, discovery documents written in heavy tech jargon you don’t speak, just to get passed to a different team once the contract is signed.

People either overpay, get something that isn’t what they described, or give up. Usually they give up.

We built this company to be the translator in the middle, and to stay in the middle the entire time.

Experience

You always know what to expect

Twenty-five years of business IT sits behind that — the unglamorous kind, for organizations where the system has to work on Monday morning whether or not anyone feels like fixing it. What that history buys you isn’t faster typing. It’s judgment: knowing which shortcuts are safe to take, which requirements will change no matter what the document says, and which “small change” quietly turns into a rebuild in month four.

We also lean hard on current AI tooling across our stack. Used well, it removes the expensive, error-prone parts of the process — half-written documentation, logic nobody thought through, work that has to be redone.

How we work

How we work with you

Our conversations are casual on purpose. You get to be enthusiastic about your idea instead of performing technical fluency you don’t have — and while you’re talking, we’re gathering requirements, mapping screens, and working out how your data needs to relate to itself. All done with casual, friendly conversations.

You can be as involved as you want. Some clients bring their own designs, wireframes, tables, and data relationships, and save time and money. Some bring an idea and a budget and want to watch it happen. Neither is the wrong answer.

We don’t rush you off the phone, and we don’t hand you to another team.

The same person stays with you until your app is published live.

About

The middle person you've been missing

!WackySolutions LLC is a creative technology company. We exist for one specific person: the one with a genuinely good app idea and no idea what to do next.

Why we started

There is a gap in this industry, and a lot of good ideas fall into it.

On one side you have people with real ideas — a way to fix something they deal with every day, a business they've run for years that should have an app. On the other side you have development firms who quote in story points, hand you a discovery document written in a language you don't speak, and pass you to a different team once the contract is signed.

The people in the middle either overpay, get something that isn't what they described, or give up. Usually they give up.

We built this company to be the translator in the middle, and to stay in the middle the entire time.

What twenty-five years buys you

We have been building business IT solutions for over twenty-five years — the unglamorous kind, for organizations where systems have to actually work on Monday morning. That history matters less for the code and more for the judgment: knowing which shortcuts are safe, which requirements are going to change, and which "small change" is going to cost you a rebuild in month four.

We also lean hard on current AI tooling across our stack. Used well, it removes the expensive, error-prone parts of the process — half-written documentation, logic nobody thought through, work that has to be redone. That is a cost that should come down. For a lot of firms it hasn't.

How we work with you

Our conversations are casual on purpose. You get to be enthusiastic about your idea instead of performing technical fluency you don't have — and while you're talking, we're gathering requirements, mapping screens, and working out how your data needs to relate to itself. You'll find out later how much got decided during what felt like a friendly chat.

You can be as involved as you want. Some clients bring their own designs, wireframes, tables, and data relationships, and their price reflects it. Some bring an idea and a budget and want to watch it happen. Neither is the wrong answer.

What doesn't change: we don't rush you off the phone, and we don't hand you to another team once your account is signed. The people you talk to are the people building it.

Who we're for

  • First-time founders with an idea and no technical background
  • Small business owners whose operation should have an app by now
  • People who've had one quote from a development firm and found it alarming
  • Anyone who's been told their idea is "simple to build" and suspects that isn't true

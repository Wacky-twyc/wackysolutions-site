About

We are the translator in the middle

!WackySolutions is an app development company. We are here for the person with a genuinely good app idea and no idea what to do next.

Twenty-five years of business IT, the unglamorous kind, for organizations where systems have to actually work on Monday morning. That history matters less for the code and more for the judgment.

About

Why we started

We think anybody should be able to have an app if they want one

Apps can entertain people, automate the annoying stuff, keep a business organized, make scheduling easier, connect a community, sell something, teach something. If you’ve been thinking, “There should be an app for that,” we’re here for it.

Big idea. Small idea. Business idea. Wacky idea.

If you want to build it, we want to hear about it.

Experience

You always know what to expect

Twenty-five years of building business solutions sit behind that. What that experience delivers isn’t faster coding, but judgment: knowing what questions to ask before development begins, recognizing when one decision will affect something three screens away, and understanding which details need to be worked through now so they don’t become expensive changes later.

It also means knowing that what looks like a small change on the surface can affect much more underneath, and planning for that before it becomes a problem.

We also use current AI tools throughout our process. Not to replace experience, but to make better use of it. AI helps us organize requirements, strengthen documentation, catch inconsistencies earlier, and reduce repetitive work, leaving more time for the decisions, conversations, and problem-solving that actually shape your app.

How we work

How we work with you

Our conversations are casual on purpose. You get to be enthusiastic about your idea without worrying about using the right technical words. While you’re talking, we’re listening for what your app needs to do, mapping out the screens, gathering requirements, and figuring out how all the information in your app needs to connect and work together.

All through casual, friendly conversations.

You can be as involved as you want. Some clients come with their own designs, wireframes, tables, and a clear idea of how everything should connect, which can save both time and money. Others come with an idea, a budget, and a desire to watch it take shape. Neither approach is the wrong one.

We don’t rush you off the phone, and we don’t hand you off to another team.

The same person stays with you from the first conversation until your app is published and live.

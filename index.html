What we actually do

Three problems, one company

Most people with an app idea get stuck on one of these. You'd normally hire three different firms. You don't have to.

Stand up the business

We form the entity your app will live inside, set up the accounting structure so revenue has somewhere legitimate to land, and keep you on the right side of state and federal filings.

Design and build the app

Wireframes first, so you're making decisions about something you can see. Then the build — iOS and Android — with an AI-assisted stack that keeps cost down instead of padding hours.

Get it published

Developer accounts, store listings, review submissions, the rejection letters nobody warns you about. We handle the paperwork and the resubmissions.

How it goes

From "I have an idea" to "it's live"

  1. A conversation, not an intake form

    Tell us about the idea the way you'd tell a friend. We're listening for the things that become requirements. You don't need vocabulary you don't have.

  2. Wireframes you can react to

    Screens in order, laid out so you can say "no, that button goes there." Changing your mind at this stage is free — that's the entire point of doing it first.

  3. Your business entity, set up properly

    Formed in the right state with the right structure, so the app has somewhere to earn and you have something to file.

  4. The build

    We build it. You're welcome to watch closely or check in weekly — some clients want a standing call, some want to be surprised. Both are fine.

  5. Submission and launch

    App Store and Google Play. We handle the developer accounts, the listings, the review process, and the fixes if reviewers push back.

Why we're different

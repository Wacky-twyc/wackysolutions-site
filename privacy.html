Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This policy explains what !WackySolutions LLC collects when you use wackysolutions.org, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Who we are

!WackySolutions LLC ("!WackySolutions," "we," "us") is a Georgia limited liability company based in Atlanta, Georgia. We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

What we collect

Information you give us

When you submit our contact form or email or call us, we receive what you choose to send: your name, email address, phone number if you provide it, the stage your project is at, and the description of your idea. We do not ask for sensitive categories of personal information, and we ask that you not send them.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider records standard server and security logs when a page is requested. These may include your IP address, browser type, the pages requested, and timestamps. This is ordinary infrastructure logging used to keep the site running and to defend it from abuse.

Cookies

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies, and does not use third-party tracking pixels. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy before doing so and will describe what is collected.

Third-party resources

This site loads web fonts and animation libraries from third-party content delivery networks. When your browser requests those files, the provider receives your IP address and browser information as a normal consequence of the request. We do not share any other information with them and receive nothing back about you.

Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and discuss your project
  • To prepare quotes and proposals you have asked for
  • To deliver services under a contract with you
  • To keep the website secure, available, and free of abuse
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not add you to marketing lists you did not ask to join.

Confidentiality of your idea

People send us business ideas. We treat the substance of what you describe as confidential and use it only to evaluate and discuss the work you are asking us to do. If you want a signed non-disclosure agreement before you tell us anything, say so and we will sign one first.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us operate — our website host, our email provider, and, where a project requires it, development partners bound by confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to establish or defend legal claims.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are kept for up to twenty-four months and then deleted. Records connected to a client engagement are kept for as long as the relationship lasts and afterwards for the period required by tax, accounting, and contract-limitation rules.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. We limit access to enquiry data to the people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — so please do not send passwords, financial account numbers, or government identification numbers through the contact form.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing, or to receive your information in a portable format.

Residents of California, and of other US states with comprehensive privacy laws, have the rights described above and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

To make a request, email info@wackysolutions.org. We will respond within the time the applicable law allows, and we may need to verify your identity first.

Children

This website is intended for adults conducting business. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, email us and we will delete it.

International visitors

We operate in the United States and information you send us is processed there. Privacy laws in the United States may differ from those where you live.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the revised version on this page and update the date above. Material changes will be described at the top of the page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or about your information go to info@wackysolutions.org, or call 404-409-6050.
